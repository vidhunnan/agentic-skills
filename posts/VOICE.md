# Voice

**Status: captured** · 2026-08-30
**Evidence:** five LinkedIn posts by the author, 1–3 years old, supplied 2026-08-30.

## Read this first

The samples are **announcement posts** — workshop promos, event recaps, a year in
review, community thank-yous. None is a build-log or an exploration post, which is
the genre these skills exist for.

So they are used two ways at once. The **person** in them carries over. The
**packaging** does not. Both columns below come from the same five posts.

Where a rule below has no evidence line, it came from the author directly and
outranks the samples.

---

## Carries over

Observed in the samples, and wanted.

**Credit people by name.** Four of five posts name the specific people involved and
say what they did. This is the most consistent single habit in the evidence, and it
is the first thing to preserve. If someone else was in the work, they are in the
post.

**Real numbers, and only real ones.** "41 sessions across 3 countries", "top 20% of
mentors". Where a number exists it is stated plainly. Where none exists, none is
invented. This already matches the family's sourcing rule.

**Enabling framing.** The reader is someone being helped to level up, not an
audience being performed to. *"help teams shift from manual mode to system-first
thinking"*, *"there's so much power in building smarter"*. A post is for the reader's
benefit, not the author's standing.

**Direct address.** *"If you're a design agency or product team trying to scale
efficiently…"*. The posts speak to a specific person with a specific problem rather
than to everyone.

**Thesis first.** The strongest sample opens on the claim, not on context or setup.
The first line is the argument.

**Short paragraphs, one idea each,** with a blank line between. Never a wall.

**Concrete lists.** Specific, nameable things: *Advanced Figma workflows.
Tokenization strategies. Design–dev sync.* Keep the habit of listing specifics.
Drop the emoji markers it used.

**Warmth.** Not clinical, not detached. A person wrote it.

**A close that invites.** Every sample ends by opening a door rather than trailing
off. In a build-log post this is a question or an open thread, not a call to action.

## Stripped

Observed in the samples, and explicitly not wanted. Author's instruction beats the
evidence here.

| Strip | Seen in | Instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Em dashes** | 4 of 5 | A full stop, or a comma. Never the dash |
| **Hashtags** | 5 of 5, four to seven each | **None. Not in the draft, not suggested alongside it** |
| **Emoji as bullets** | ✅ and 🏅 and 📈 markers | Plain bullets |
| "Excited to announce", "I'm thrilled", "truly honoured" | 2 of 5 | Say the thing itself |
| "Unlock the full potential", "raise the bar", "don't miss out", "treasure trove", "massive shoutout", "unforgettable" | 3 of 5 | Plain words |
| Title Case Headline With Emoji Prefix | 1 of 5 | A sentence |
| Stacked exclamation marks | Heaviest in the year-in-review | At most one per post. In a build-log, zero |

**Emoji: sparing, and never structural.** At most one in a post, and only where it
is doing something a word could not. Never as a list marker, never as a header
prefix.

## Register: announcement vs build-log

The samples are the announcement register. The build-log register is quieter and
more specific: it names a real thing that happened, says what broke, and does not
sell anything. The warmth and the direct address carry across both. The
enthusiasm markers do not.

*(The build-log register has no samples yet. When the author writes one they like,
it belongs here as evidence, and this section gets rewritten from it.)*

## Banned moves

Hard-coded for every post, before any of the above applies. Not derived from the
samples.

- No em dashes.
- No hashtags.
- No "excited to announce", "thrilled to share", "so proud to".
- No emoji bullets, no emoji header prefixes.
- No rhetorical-question openers.
- No "here's what I learned:" listicle scaffolding.
- No invented metrics. A number appears only if a source contains it.
- No manufactured struggle arc.
- None of: game-changer, unlock, leverage, dive deep, supercharge, seamless.
