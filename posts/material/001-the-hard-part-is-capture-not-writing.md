# The hard part of building in public is not writing the post

Type: reframe · Captured: 2026-08-30 · Status: drafted
Expires: —

Artifacts: *(none)*

## What happened

The brief was a skill that writes social posts about project work. Over one
brainstorm it became a family of five, and the member that turned out to matter
most does not write posts at all. It captures material.

The turn came from asking what happens in a project with no changelog and no
decision log, which is most projects. The answer exposed the real problem: even
where a record exists, it holds what shipped and why it was chosen. It does not
hold the third attempt that failed, or what the flow looked like before. Those are
the parts a post is built from, and they survive about a day.

## Why it's interesting to someone who wasn't here

It relocates the problem. Everyone treats posting about your work as a writing
problem and reaches for a writing tool. It is a capture problem, and by the time
you feel the writing problem the inputs are already gone.

The sharp version: git keeps the before-state of your code. It keeps nothing of
the before-state of anything you would actually want to show a person. The
screenshot of the old flow, the failing output, the doc as it read last week. All
overwritten, none recoverable.
