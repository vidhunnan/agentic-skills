# Research — What did we learn?

**Question:** What did we learn?
**Tense:** past
**Status:** evidence — observation kept separate from interpretation
**Written by:** human
**Lifecycle:** Append-only. Findings don't expire, but their **premises** do — note when a study's context no longer holds rather than deleting it.

What we actually observed, kept distinct from what we concluded from it.

That split is the whole point of this tier. *"7 of 9 participants scrolled past the CTA"* is an **observation**. *"Users ignore the CTA"* is a **claim about why**. Collapse them into one folder and the claim hardens into folklore — cited a year later as fact, by people who never saw the session.

## What goes here

- One file per study or input: interviews, usability sessions, competitive scans, analytics pulls, support-ticket reviews.
- Method, participants, and the raw observations with counts.
- Your interpretation — **clearly labelled as interpretation**, and separable from the observations above it.
- Confidence, honestly stated. Five participants is five participants.

## What does NOT go here

- What the design should therefore be — that's `../briefs/` or `../explorations/`.
- A conclusion with no observation behind it. If nobody watched anything, it's a hunch, and hunches belong in `../../docs/concepts/`.
- Rounds of design iteration — that's `../explorations/`. **Research is about the problem and the people; explorations are about the solution.**
