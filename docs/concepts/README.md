# Concepts — What are we even trying to build?

**Question:** What are we even trying to build?
**Tense:** future
**Status:** hypothesis
**Written by:** human
**Lifecycle:** Disposable. Delete it or graduate it into a PRD. Never cite a concept as a decision.

The whiteboard. Half-formed thinking, argued with yourself in public. Nothing here is a commitment, and an agent must never treat it as one.

## What goes here

- Ideas you're turning over: "what if skills could compose?"
- Hypotheses you haven't tested.
- Sketches of features that may never be built.
- Reasoning you want to be able to pick back up in a month.

## What does NOT go here

- Anything settled — that's `../decisions/`.
- Anything you've committed to building — graduate it into `../prds/`.
- Anything that already shipped — that's `../../changelog/`.

## Template

Copy `_TEMPLATE.md`. When a concept survives contact with reality, graduate it into a PRD and delete it from here.
