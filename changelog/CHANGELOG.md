### Changed
- **Four supersessions closed — and one record superseded for being false, not for being reversed** (`e05015d`) — 0002→0008, 0004→0005→0009, 0006→0010, 0001→0011, and repo [0016](../docs/decisions/0016-site-content-is-data-driven-from-one-source.md)→0023. **0016 was not reversed on principle; it was false as written.** It claimed *"a single content module; the components render it rather than embedding copy"* while around fifty user-facing strings — every headline, every intro paragraph — had always been literals in components, which is what let the site say *"six skills"* in six places long after there were eleven. [0023](../docs/decisions/0023-site-content-is-split-by-kind-data-vs-prose.md) records the split that was actually in force: repeated and countable content is data, section prose is not, and every count is derived so no sentence states a number a human typed. It also names its own limit — **the boundary is a convention, not a mechanism**; neither `next build` nor `tsc --noEmit` catches a literal count. On the five superseded records **only the `**Status:**` line and an appended, dated `## Follow-up` entry were touched** — 33 lines added, 8 removed, no frozen body text. Both indexes were rebuilt, and the repo index's standing claim that *"the one supersession crosses tiers"* is no longer true.
