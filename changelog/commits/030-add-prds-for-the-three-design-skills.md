## Commit message

Rewrites the three incubation PRDs to the house template — the nine sections, the
deliberately-unnumbered Non-goals, the per-surface trigger table ahead of the
numbered requirements.

Three pieces of content did not survive the move, all of them point-in-time claims
about a private backlog rather than statements about the problem:

- design-explore's "this skill conflicts with ADR 0021 ... it ships in a consuming
  repo first" — replaced by the gap it actually fills and a citation of ADR 0022,
  which resolved it.
- design-explore's "the library has no generative design skill at all" — true when
  written, false the moment this shipped.
- design-critique's problem section, which was a list of the four files that
  referenced a skill that didn't exist yet — replaced with the two ways model design
  feedback actually fails.

Requirements gained the rigor-pass behaviour: check mode (R11/R15/R15), the write
gate (R7/R10/R12), tier-scoped writes (R13), the candidates-not-a-record notice
(R14), and every critique finding citing the intent it violates (R14).

Risks are stated honestly rather than tidily. design-explore names erosion of the
ADR 0022 conditions as its largest risk and says the mitigation is partial.
design-critique keeps the concept doc's open question — whether a model can review
craft at all — and concedes only the constraint-checking half is verifiable.

## Changes in detail

### `docs/prds/design-language.md` (new)

- R1–R11. R11 is new: `check` mode must report the resolved path, every language doc
  found, its unanswered-section count, and readiness for `/design-explore`, with
  zero writes and no questions asked.
- R7 was strengthened from "show back before writing" to a single confirmation gate
  that must be accepted before anything is written; R8 now requires aborting if
  untouched sections changed since they were read.
- §1 states the two skills this document gates — `design-critique` by ADR 0021,
  `design-explore` by condition 1 of ADR 0022.
- §8 adds a risk the source PRD did not carry: being the ADR 0022 gate creates
  pressure to produce a thin doc purely to unblock generation, and the mitigation is
  weak — `check` makes a thin doc visible but nothing prevents one.

### `docs/prds/design-explore.md` (new)

- R1–R15. R13–R15 are new: writes confined to the explorations tier, the
  candidates-not-a-record notice plus never overwriting a prior round, and `check`
  mode.
- §1 rewritten. The incubation paragraph is gone; in its place, the gap in the stack
  (brief and language on one side, exploration-log and design-decisions on the
  other, nothing where options come into existence) and the ADR 0021 → 0022 history
  that permits the skill.
- §8 leads with erosion of the ADR 0022 conditions as the largest risk, and says
  plainly that it is structural rather than behavioural — each condition costs the
  user something in the moment — and that stating them in the skill body mitigates
  but does not eliminate it. The trigger collision with `exploration-log` is
  recorded as a risk with its scoping mitigation.

### `docs/prds/design-critique.md` (new)

- R1–R15. R14 and R15 are new: every finding must cite the intent it violates in its
  `Against:` line or be dropped, and `check` mode reports the gate without
  performing a review.
- §1 rewritten from backlog bookkeeping into the two failure modes — flattery, and
  the generic checklist — plus the quieter bias toward adding rather than removing.
- §8 keeps the concept doc's unresolved question rather than resolving it by
  assertion: whether a model can review craft at all is still open, and the honest
  answer recorded here is that only the constraint-checking half is verifiable.
- §9 adds the corresponding v2 question — whether to ship only the mechanically
  checkable pass, the scoping-down the concept doc anticipated.
