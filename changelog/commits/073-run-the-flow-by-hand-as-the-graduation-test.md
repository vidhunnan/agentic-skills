  That is the exact failure the family is designed to prevent, and it appeared in the first
  draft written.
- **Five of five material items had an entry under "what I'd have to check before posting
  it".** The section is not decoration.
- **`post-angles` was never reached.** Material went straight to draft, because the typed
  backlog had already done the surfacing. Logged as item 005 and carried into that skill's
  PRD as a named risk rather than left as an opinion.
- **The best finding was unplanned.** Pre-filling the card direction from
  `design/system/palette.md` meant inheriting the palette's **semantic** roles, where redline
  means *do not treat this as settled*. The frame therefore labels itself a hypothesis in the
  design system's own language — the honesty rule reaching the visual layer without anyone
  designing that.
