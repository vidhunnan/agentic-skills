They moved 21px depending on which record was showing. The caption under the card

Three caption lines are now reserved, and the caption is clamped to three so a
longer one cannot push them either. A clamp hides text silently, so the suite
asserts no caption is clipped — write a fourth line and the tests tell you instead
of the page quietly eating it.

Measured against the bottom of the card, not the viewport. A viewport-relative

clicking the button scrolls it into view. Two of my own measurements today were
wrong that way before I checked what I was measuring against.

The new test also had to wait for hydration before measuring. The controls are
mount-gated — they do not exist until React hydrates — so reading them immediately
after goto returned null, which only showed up on the slower mobile profile.

### `website/components/Hero.module.css` (modified, +14)

- **Three caption lines are reserved**, and the caption is **clamped to three** so a
  longer one cannot push the arrows the other way. Both halves are needed: reserving
  alone fixes the short case, clamping alone fixes the long one.
- **The clamp's own hazard is closed in the same commit.** *"A clamp hides text
  silently, so the suite asserts no caption is clipped — write a fourth line and the
  tests tell you instead of the page quietly eating it."* This is the house pattern
  again (entries 041, 047, 051): a change that could fail invisibly gets a test that
  makes it fail loudly.

### `website/tests/specimen.spec.ts` (+53)

Two methodology notes in the message are the durable content of this entry, and neither
is about the arrows:

- **Measure against the thing, not the viewport.** A viewport-relative reading reported
  the arrows moving **514px** when what actually moved was the page — clicking the
  button scrolls it into view. The new assertion measures against the bottom of the
  card. The message logs the frequency honestly: *"Two of my own measurements today were
  wrong that way before I checked what I was measuring against."*
- **Mount-gated controls need a hydration wait.** The controls do not exist until React
  hydrates, so reading them immediately after `goto` returned `null`. *"which only
  showed up on the slower mobile profile"* — a race the Chromium project would have
  passed. This is the first recorded cost of the page-wide mount-gating rule that
  entries 045, 046 and 055 all rely on, and it lands in the test layer rather than the
  product.
