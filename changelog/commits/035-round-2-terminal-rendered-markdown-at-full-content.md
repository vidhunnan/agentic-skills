## Commit message

Round 1's finding was that its own comps were too thin to test anything but register:
3 of 14 skills, 1 of 6 specimens, no nav, no footer, ~6.5KB against the real page's
104KB. The owner's words were "the content of the website feels empty" and "the html
feels more of POC", and both were correct.

So round 2 tests the one thing round 1 could not: does the register survive a full
page? All 14 skills, all 9 matrix rows, six sections, nav and footer. 19KB.

The direction, made concrete: markdown syntax is present but recessive. The `##`
before a heading, the `**` around bold, the pipes in a table and the ``` around a
fence are all rendered in --mute rather than hidden. That is the strongest available
reading of the hard constraint "records are shown verbatim, never restyled" —
showing the source markers IS verbatim, where rendering them into styled components
would not be. The page and the artifact become the same material, which is the reason
the owner gave for choosing this direction.

Two things authored for the first time. The worked-loop section, which the current
site has nowhere: decisions-logger from "you decided something" through reading the
evidence, asking what it can't find, writing the record, registering the rule, and
offering again next session unasked. Step 4 is named as the one nothing else does.
And the install section as a sequence rather than a menu of fourteen equal commands.

Constraints verified: no JS at all, so it reads with JS off and nothing types itself.
No CDN or external dependency. Palette copied verbatim from globals.css, both modes;
--scrim and --scrim-solid are unused because the file bar sits on solid ground.

No verdict recorded here — that belongs to exploration-log once it has been looked at.
