website's SKILL_GROUPS. Fourteen skills now, in both README count strings.

built when four were, because exploration-log never got its marker. Reconciled to
seven, and the roadmap now reflects what actually happened rather than what was
planned:

- design-explore absorbed design-brainstorm and design-directions. The split did
  not survive contact with the work, so the count is nineteen, not twenty.
- design-language was added to Frame it. The original list had no slot for it; the
  gap only became visible once design-critique needed something to review against.
- The "line these skills do not cross" paragraph keeps its argument and gains the
  ADR 0022 exception, scoped to explorations and named as conditional.

craft at all is still open — the shipped skill hedges by hard-gating on written
intent and dropping findings that cannot quote a rule, and what to watch is whether
the perceptual half earns its place. And the kill criterion is now weaker than it
was: a tier filled by a generator is not evidence anyone chose to record a rejected
direction, so the test is the verdicts, not the file count.
